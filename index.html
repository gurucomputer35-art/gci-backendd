About us

Who we are and what we stand for.

Welcome to Guru Computer Institute

Guru Computer Institute, on Gurudwara Road in Raja Ka Tajpur, Bijnor, helps students, job seekers and working people learn practical computer skills. Our courses cover computer basics, office software, accounting with Tally and GST, Excel and complete computer diplomas, and are taught in simple language that is useful in real jobs.

We believe the best way to learn computers is by using them. That is why every class includes hands-on practice, and every student gets personal attention from our teachers.

🎯

Our mission

To make quality computer education affordable and easy to understand for everyone in our community.

🔭

Our vision

A skilled, confident and job-ready generation that can grow in the digital world.

🛠️

Our approach

Learn by doing: more practical work, less theory, and simple explanations in your own language.

What makes us different

✅ Job-oriented courses

✅ Practical computer lab

Daily hands-on practice on real software.
