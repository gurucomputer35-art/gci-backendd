About Us

Know who we are and what we stand for.

Welcome to Guru Computer Institute

Guru Computer Institute, on Gurudwara Road in Raja Ka Tajpur, Bijnor, is a computer training institute that helps students, job seekers and working professionals learn practical computer skills. From Basic Computer and MS Office to Tally Prime with GST, Advanced Excel, DCA and ADCA, our courses are simple to understand and useful in real jobs.

We believe the best way to learn computers is by using them. That is why every class has hands-on practice, and every student gets personal attention from our teachers.

🎯

Our Mission

To make quality computer education affordable and easy to understand for everyone in our community.

🔭

Our Vision

To build a skilled, confident and job-ready generation that can grow in the digital world.

🛠️

Our Approach

Learn by doing: more practical work, less theory, and simple explanations in your own language.
